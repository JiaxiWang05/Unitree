## 0. Bottom line

- **No Durham Open Day falls before the next few deadlines, and approval also takes time.** HRI 2027 LBR (8 Dec 2026), CHI 2027 Posters (21 Jan 2027) and ICSR 2027 papers (15 Feb 2027) all close before any 2027 Open Day or post-offer visit day. You cannot submit Open Day data to any of them.
- **First realistic data collection:** the spring 2027 **Undergraduate Post-offer Visit Days**. The 2027 dates are not announced. They are usually in March and April, and Computer Science's 2026 days were Sat 14 Mar and Sat 25 Apr 2026. Ethics approval would have to be in place by about February 2027, so the ethics application should go in **now (Nov–Dec 2026)**.
- **Main study:** the **June 2027 Undergraduate Open Days**. The 2027 dates are not announced; the 2026 days were Fri 26 and Sat 27 June. Write it up as an **HRI 2028 full paper**: on last year's pattern the abstract is due in mid-September (11 Sep 2026 for HRI 2027). Alternatives are **THRI** or **IJSR** (rolling submission) or RO-MAN 2028.
- **Good fit for pilot or deployment lessons:** IJSR topical collection *"Failing Forward: Design and Deployment Lessons from Real-World HRI"* (deadline **1 June 2027**), CUI 2027 Short papers/WiP (Swansea, deadline "TBC April 2027"), and the RO-MAN 2027 LBR (not announced yet; last year it was 12 June).
- **Things you could still do before Christmas without Open Day data:** an HRI 2027 Demo or a Student Design Competition entry (theme "Robots in public spaces"), both due **20 Nov 2026 AoE**.
